# 📰 the-page-news

A digital news platform dedicated to investigative reporting and real-time updates on global energy sectors and political affairs.

## 🚀 Overview
**the-page-news** serves as a lightweight, web-based news portal. Our current focus is on the evolving energy crisis and uncovering industry-specific scandals through transparent reporting.

## 📂 Project Structure
* `index.html`: The main landing page and news feed.
* `article-s/`: (In Progress) A collection of investigative deep-dives.
* `assets/`: (Planned) Imagery and data visualizations for reports.
