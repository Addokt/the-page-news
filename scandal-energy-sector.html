SCANDAL IN THE ENERGY SECTOR: GH¢8.8 BILLION HOLE EXPOSED

By Adam Ibrahim

The 2023 Auditor-General's Report reveals systemic corruption and gross negligence within state enterprises. While total irregularities amounted to GH¢8.8 billion, the bulk was in the energy sector.

1. ECG: Procurement Chaos

ECG reportedly awarded 512 contracts through single-sourcing in 2023, representing 72.83% of all contracts.

2. GNPC: Misapplied Funds

GNPC paid GH¢4.9 million for projects lacking Parliamentary approval.
